ForgeCRA · Design partner pilot

We run the supplier evidence workflow for you, by hand, for 90 days.

There is no self-serve platform yet. You get the process executed properly, and you help shape what gets built.

Who it is for 01

Manufacturers who place products with digital elements on the EU market and who currently rely on email, spreadsheets or one-off questionnaires to get SBOMs from upstream suppliers.

If your main pain is answering other people's questionnaires — you are primarily a component or module supplier — this pilot is not the right fit. The free supplier cohort is.

What you get in the 90 days 02
  1. Supplier map. We work from your actual upstream list (components, modules, firmware, BSPs, critical OSS where relevant).
  2. Structured requests. We request machine-readable SBOMs (CycloneDX or SPDX) in a consistent form, instead of open-ended email chains.
  3. Quality scoring. Every submission is checked against a practical minimum bar (identity, versions, key fields, basic completeness). You see what is usable and what is not.
  4. Supplier attestation. Where we get a usable SBOM, we obtain a clear attestation from the supplier that it relates to the relevant product/version at a stated date. We quality-score, timestamp and record it. The supplier attests; you remain the manufacturer under the CRA.
  5. Evidence pack. A clean set of attested SBOMs and status for the suppliers in scope — something you can show internally, to auditors, or to customers without living in an inbox.
  6. Working process. A repeatable way to request, chase, score and file the next round of updates.
What we do, and do not do 03

We do

  • Run the collection and follow-up with your suppliers
  • Score what comes back
  • Obtain and record attestations
  • Keep you informed on response rates and quality
  • Document gaps that remain

We do not

  • Generate SBOMs by reverse-engineering binaries (that is a different class of tool)
  • Replace your existing binary-analysis or vulnerability-management tools
  • Claim CRA certification or conformity
  • Guarantee every supplier will respond
The number that matters 04

We measure response rate and usable attested SBOM rate from your supplier list against your current baseline — whatever your email and questionnaire results look like today.

If we cannot show a clear improvement inside the 90 days, the pilot has not worked, and we will say so in the debrief.

After the 90 days 06

You receive the evidence pack, the status of every supplier in scope, and a short written summary of what improved and what remained blocked.

There is no automatic renewal. If you want to continue, we discuss scope and price for a further period, or for early access to the software when it exists. If you do not, the engagement ends. You keep everything produced during the pilot.

How it runs 07
  1. Short scoping call (suppliers in scope, products, current process, success criteria).
  2. Agreement and kick-off.
  3. Weekly or fortnightly status (what was requested, what came back, scores, blockers).
  4. Final evidence pack and debrief at day 90.

You remain the point of authority with your suppliers. We do the operational work and the quality gate, under your introduction where needed.

Practical constraints 08
  • We take a small number of design partners at a time (currently up to five).
  • The workflow is run by us, by hand. That is intentional while we prove the process.
  • Suppliers are not charged. You are.
